Information we collect

DCM - Quick Sort does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information as part of its normal operation. The application does not require an account, registration, advertising identifier, analytics service, telemetry service or location permission.

Files and folders

The application works with files and folders that you choose on your computer. It may list files, preview supported files, read a file when you explicitly choose to view it as text, rename files, copy or move files, create destination folders, and open files or folders using Windows.

Your files and their contents are not uploaded by DCM - Quick Sort to Cyber Planet or to a DCM server.

File previews

Quick Sort may use Windows and preview components installed on your computer to display supported file types. Those components may be supplied by Microsoft or other software vendors and may be subject to their own terms. DCM - Quick Sort itself does not transmit previewed file contents to Cyber Planet.

Internet access

The current version does not require an internet connection for its normal file-sorting workflow.
