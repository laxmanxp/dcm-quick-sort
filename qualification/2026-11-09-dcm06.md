Cross-volume failure continues into the existing exclusive-create, verified-copy/source-retirement fallback. The non-Windows helper refuses the native fast path instead of promising unsupported atomic behavior. No preview, layout, printing or Walk migration change was made.

This closes the reproduced occupied-destination regression; it does not claim all file-operation or filesystem-race cases are certified. Visible normal-app Move/conflict testing remains in the later UI steps.
